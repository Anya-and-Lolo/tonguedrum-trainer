Jianpu quick guide How to read numbered music

What is Jianpu?

Jianpu is a numbered music notation system. The numbers 1-7 represent the notes of the scale, so the same melody can be reused in different keys.

  • 1 = Do
  • 2 = Re
  • 3 = Mi
  • 4 = Fa
  • 5 = So
  • 6 = La
  • 7 = Ti

0 is a rest: stay silent for one beat. Several 0s in a row make a longer silence.

Rhythm

In Jianpu a plain number lasts one beat. Lines underneath make it shorter, dashes after it make it longer.

5 Regular (quarter note)No underline = one regular beat
5 Quick (eighth note)One underline = two notes in one regular beat
5 Very quick (sixteenth note)Two underlines = four notes in one regular beat
5 Half noteOne dash to the right = two beats
5 Whole noteThree dashes to the right = four beats
Octave dots
1Low 1Middle 1High 1Very high

A dot above raises the number by one octave.
A dot below lowers it by one octave.

Beats, bars and rests

Music is counted in steady beats, like tapping your foot. A bar line | gathers a few beats into a bar, usually four.

Underlines only join notes that share the same beat. So four quick notes are drawn as two joined pairs, not one long line. The break shows you where the next foot-tap lands.

63 36 21 72 17

One underline per beat. Tap your foot once for each: 63, 36, 21, 72, 17. The tall lines are bar lines.
